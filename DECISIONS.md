| 2026-09-09 | Below DISK_MIN_FREE_GB the archive stops, the logger does not | A quote row is a few hundred bytes and is what the scoreboard reads; a raw payload is a megabyte and is what fills a disk. | 2026-09-09 | `quotes` is the only table that may be pruned outright (21 days) | It is derived - every row was parsed from an archived payload, so the archive re-produces any window. `poll_log` and `markets` are kept: poll_log is the coverage record and is how gaps stay visible |
| 2026-09-09 | Dead-man switch at 20 min, plus per-venue staleness | A crash is loud and gets restarted. The failure that matters is the process that stays up and quietly stops capturing - an expired session, a venue that moved a filter - which from outside is identical to a healthy logger on a quiet night |
| 2026-09-09 | Rotation and pruning run inside run_logger, not only from cron | The requirement is surviving three weeks of neglect. Retention that works only when somebody remembers to install a cron entry does not survive neglect, it just relocates the failure. Both stay independently runnable as `python -m jobs.*` |
| 2026-09-09 | Quotes retention 14d, not 21d | Measured: raw **1.10 GB/day** gzipped + quotes **0.61 GB/day** = **1.71 GB/day** (11.7 min window, quiet midweek, no hot tier). | 2026-09-09 | TACKLES+ASSISTS = solo + with_assist + assists; box-score SOLO = solo + with_assist | Verified against ESPN, three player-games. | 2026-09-09 | nfl_player_week rebuilt from the LOCAL archive, not re-fetched | Invariant #2 collecting on its promise: widening a projection is a re-parse. 27 shards, no network, each re-normalized under its original data_version so a parser change manufactures no new version |
| 2026-09-09 | Venues disagree on the calendar day of a night game | Kalshi and nflverse date the 2026 opener 09-09 (Eastern); Polymarket says 09-10 (UTC, kickoff 00:20Z). | 2026-09-09 | Name ambiguity is broken on facts, never on similarity | "DeVonta Smith" (WR) and "Devonta Smith" (CB) collide after normalization. | 2026-09-09 | Every logged market gets a market_outcome row, mapped or not | Coverage computed over only the markets you managed to parse is not a coverage number, and the unmapped list grouped by reason is the queue that says which mapper to improve next |
| 2026-09-09 | Settlement is versioned by the data_version it was computed from | A stat correction writes a SECOND settlement row rather than editing the first. Settling a prop is when a backtest's P&L becomes real; a silently restated settlement is a silently restated track record |
| 2026-09-09 | Brief 003 coverage: kalshi 1,743/3,091 (100% of every non-future type), polymarket 1,678/4,742, 263 outcomes cross-venue | Unmapped remainder is season-long slugs, futures, quarter/half segments and oddsapi discovery rows - all recorded with reasons |

| 2026-09-09 | `last_team` is the team a player FINISHED on, chronologically | `MAX(team)` is alphabetical. 87 players changed teams during 2025, so the roster-change penalty fired on the wrong ones in both directions - Joe Flacco would have read as CLE when he finished on CIN |
| 2026-09-09 | A superseded model is superseded by a new VERSION, never by deleting rows | Predictions are immutable (invariant #6). baseline-usage-0.2's 2,098 rows stay in the store; 0.3 supersedes them, and every consumer filters by model_version. | 2026-09-09 | Flat stakes only, no Kelly | QB<->WR1 is ~0.42 and a diagonal covariance systematically over-bets a correlated slate. Correlated sizing is its own brief |
| 2026-09-09 | The mid is recorded WITH its spread, and wide markets never get a ticket | A 2-cent edge inside a 6-cent spread is a rounding artifact of a market nobody quotes tightly, not an edge. Spread rides on every ticket so the backtest can tell them apart later |

| 2026-09-09 | Baseline 0.3 edges are MODEL ERROR, not alpha - nothing published | avg net edge +0.13/contract against a real market means the model is wrong. | 2026-09-09 | `write_quotes` updated dedupe state after filtering the whole batch | Every row in one call saw the previous call's state, so two rows for the same market in one batch both wrote. Invisible for the live logger (one row per market) and wrong the moment anything batches |
| 2026-09-10 | The ~5-call Kalshi rate limit is on `/candlesticks`, NOT `/markets/orderbooks` | The batched orderbook endpoint sustained 13.6 calls/s over 18 consecutive calls with zero 429s and returns FULL L2 per book.
