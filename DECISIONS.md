| 2026-09-09 | Raw archive is ROTATED to Cloudflare R2 at 7 days, never deleted | Invariant #2 makes the archive the thing every derivation is re-runnable from. | 2026-09-09 | `raw_shards` manifest is the answer to "where is that hour?" | rel_path, sha256, bytes, state, bucket/key, uploaded/verified/deleted timestamps. Without it a rotated archive is a set of files nobody can locate |
| 2026-09-09 | Below DISK_MIN_FREE_GB the archive stops, the logger does not | A quote row is a few hundred bytes and is what the scoreboard reads; a raw payload is a megabyte and is what fills a disk. A logger that dies on a full disk loses the hours it exists to capture. Recorded in `source_health`, degradation is visible not silent |
| 2026-09-09 | `quotes` is the only table that may be pruned outright (21 days) | It is derived - every row was parsed from an archived payload, so the archive re-produces any window. `poll_log` and `markets` are kept: poll_log is the coverage record and is how gaps stay visible |
| 2026-09-09 | Dead-man switch at 20 min, plus per-venue staleness | A crash is loud and gets restarted. The failure that matters is the process that stays up and quietly stops capturing - an expired session, a venue that moved a filter - which from outside is identical to a healthy logger on a quiet night |
| 2026-09-09 | Rotation and pruning run inside run_logger, not only from cron | The requirement is surviving three weeks of neglect. Retention that works only when somebody remembers to install a cron entry does not survive neglect, it just relocates the failure. Both stay independently runnable as `python -m jobs.*` |
| 2026-09-09 | Quotes retention 14d, not 21d | Measured: raw **1.10 GB/day** gzipped + quotes **0.61 GB/day** = **1.71 GB/day** (11.7 min window, quiet midweek, no hot tier). Local ceiling with rotation at 7d and quotes at 14d: 7.7 + 8.5 = **16.2 GB steady state**, vs 26 GB free on this box. 21d put the ceiling at 20.4GB against 26GB free, inside the noise of a game-day week; 14d leaves real margin |
| 2026-09-09 | nflverse weekly stats come from the `stats_player` release, not `player_stats` | `player_stats` froze on 2025-05-07 with no 2025/2026 assets. | 2026-09-09 | The missing-2019 weekly file needs no special case | It is an artifact of the frozen release. `stats_player_week_2019.parquet` exists on `stats_player` like every other season; the workaround was for a release that stopped being maintained |

| 2026-09-09 | A new dated copy is stored only when the BYTES change | Daily dated snapshots of 27 seasons would be ~265GB/year and would bury the handful of real stat corrections among 364 identical copies. But measured receptions mean 2.3-3.2 vs 3.75 reference and var/mean 1.8-2.3 vs 1.69; carries 7.5-9.3 vs 12.1. `core/distributions.py` and `tests/test_core.py` hardcode the reference values - do not trust them as measured until the sample screen is recovered |

| 2026-09-09 | R2 rotation is live and the wire path is verified, not just unit-tested | `--check` preflight round-tripped a real object (upload, head, read-back, sha256, delete) and a full cycle moved a real 2.9MB shard: verified, local copy reclaimed, manifest state=remote. | 2026-09-09 | `local_shards()` sees every archived file, not only `.jsonl.gz` | The extension filter made `--status` report "exempt: 0 shards" while 566MB of exempt nflverse corpus sat on disk - the one line whose job is to prove that corpus is protected. | 2026-09-09 | The healthcheck ping is emitted from the watchdog, gated on the dead-man switch | Pinging from the poll loop would create two code paths answering "is this capturing?" separately, and the failure that matters is where they disagree - a logger reassuring an external monitor while its own switch is tripped |

| 2026-09-09 | Liveness only considers venues this process is running | poll_log keeps history forever, so a venue switched off last week would otherwise read as permanently stale and silence the external monitor for good |
| 2026-09-09 | A single stale venue suppresses the ping | Partial capture is not capture. One venue dropping out of discovery while others keep the global timestamp fresh is exactly the quiet failure the switch exists for |
| 2026-09-09 | TACKLES+ASSISTS = solo + with_assist + assists; box-score SOLO = solo + with_assist | Verified against ESPN, three player-games. | 2026-09-09 | nfl_player_week rebuilt from the LOCAL archive, not re-fetched | Invariant #2 collecting on its promise: widening a projection is a re-parse. 27 shards, no network, each re-normalized under its original data_version so a parser change manufactures no new version |
| 2026-09-09 | Venues disagree on the calendar day of a night game | Kalshi and nflverse date the 2026 opener 09-09 (Eastern); Polymarket says 09-10 (UTC, kickoff 00:20Z). Keyed on the venue's own date, one game forks into two weeks or fails to resolve. `game_for` matches the team pair exactly and the date within a day |
| 2026-09-09 | Name ambiguity is broken on facts, never on similarity | "DeVonta Smith" (WR) and "Devonta Smith" (CB) collide after normalization. Tiebreak order: recorded a snap that season, then roster status. Still ambiguous = unmapped with a reason. There is no fuzzy matching anywhere in the system |
| 2026-09-09 | Every logged market gets a market_outcome row, mapped or not | Coverage computed over only the markets you managed to parse is not a coverage number, and the unmapped list grouped by reason is the queue that says which mapper to improve next |
| 2026-09-09 | Settlement is versioned by the data_version it was computed from | A stat correction writes a SECOND settlement row rather than editing the first. Settling a prop is when a backtest's P&L becomes real; a silently restated settlement is a silently restated track record |
| 2026-09-09 | Brief 003 coverage: kalshi 1,743/3,091 (100% of every non-future type), polymarket 1,678/4,742, 263 outcomes cross-venue | Unmapped remainder is season-long slugs, futures, quarter/half segments and oddsapi discovery rows - all recorded with reasons |
| 2026-09-09 | nflverse `gametime` is US/Eastern, not UTC | Stamping it as UTC put every kickoff 4-5 hours early. | 2026-09-09 | Shrink toward position AND role, never position alone | An RB1 and a third-string back are both "RB". | 2026-09-09 | `last_team` is the team a player FINISHED on, chronologically | `MAX(team)` is alphabetical. 87 players changed teams during 2025, so the roster-change penalty fired on the wrong ones in both directions - Joe Flacco would have read as CLE when he finished on CIN |
| 2026-09-09 | A superseded model is superseded by a new VERSION, never by deleting rows | Predictions are immutable (invariant #6). baseline-usage-0.2's 2,098 rows stay in the store; 0.3 supersedes them, and every consumer filters by model_version. | 2026-09-09 | Flat stakes only, no Kelly | QB<->WR1 is ~0.42 and a diagonal covariance systematically over-bets a correlated slate. Correlated sizing is its own brief |
| 2026-09-09 | The mid is recorded WITH its spread, and wide markets never get a ticket | A 2-cent edge inside a 6-cent spread is a rounding artifact of a market nobody quotes tightly, not an edge. Spread rides on every ticket so the backtest can tell them apart later |
| 2026-09-09 | Pushes are DROPPED from Brier, not scored as 0.5 | A push returns the stake. Scoring it as a half-win rewards a model for landing exactly on a line it never took a position against |
| 2026-09-09 | Baseline 0.3 edges are MODEL ERROR, not alpha - nothing published | avg net edge +0.13/contract against a real market means the model is wrong. | 2026-09-09 | `write_quotes` updated dedupe state after filtering the whole batch | Every row in one call saw the previous call's state, so two rows for the same market in one batch both wrote. Invisible for the live logger (one row per market) and wrong the moment anything batches |
| 2026-09-10 | The ~5-call Kalshi rate limit is on `/candlesticks`, NOT `/markets/orderbooks` | The batched orderbook endpoint sustained 13.6 calls/s over 18 consecutive calls with zero 429s and returns FULL L2 per book.
